Your Conditional Access admin MFA policy may skip your custom roles

Mathew Dailey · November 25, 2026

If your Conditional Access policy for admins targets "Directory roles," check who it actually covers. CA is only enforced for built-in roles, not custom roles and not roles scoped to an Administrative Unit.

Why this gap shows up

Least privilege often means custom roles and AU-scoped helpdesk admins, especially in government tenants. Those are exactly the accounts that can quietly fall outside your phishing-resistant MFA policy.

How to close the gap

  1. Put every privileged account (built-in, custom, AU-scoped, PIM-eligible) in a dedicated security group.
  2. Target that group in the policy, alongside the Directory roles.
  3. Grant: Require authentication strength → Phishing-resistant MFA (Windows Hello for Business, FIDO2, or multifactor CBA/PIV).
  4. Exclude your break-glass accounts, then run it in Report-only first.

One more catch: registration

FIDO2 and Windows Hello for Business can't be registered from the sign-in interrupt. Get admins registered before you turn the policy on, or they're locked out of the very portals they need.

Once admins are covered, make sure the device side of your policies is just as tight: by default, devices with no compliance policy count as compliant.
