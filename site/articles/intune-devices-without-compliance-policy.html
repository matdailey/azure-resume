Devices with no compliance policy count as compliant

Mathew Dailey · November 25, 2026

Your "require compliant device" policy might be letting in devices with no compliance policy at all.

The setting most people forget

Intune has a tenant-wide setting most people set once and forget: "Mark devices with no compliance policy assigned as." The default is Compliant.

So any enrolled device that falls outside your compliance assignments (a new platform, a missed group, a device excluded by a filter) can pass a Conditional Access compliance check without being evaluated against anything.

The fix takes a minute

  1. Go to Endpoint security → Device compliance → Compliance policy settings.
  2. Set "Mark devices with no compliance policy assigned as" to Not compliant.
  3. Before flipping it, open the "Devices without compliance policy" report and assign policies to anything listed, so nobody is locked out.

While you're there

Check "Compliance status validity period." The default is 30 days (range 1–120). A device that doesn't report within that window becomes noncompliant, so shorter is stricter.

If a device that should pass is getting blocked instead, read three lines of dsregcmd before you re-enroll it.
